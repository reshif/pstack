A session workspace built around the task, its path, and the evidence behind each step.

  • Workflow: the recorded path, including parallel work and retries.
  • Activity: your prompts and the agent’s actions, grouped into readable turns.
  • Evidence: the results that support each outcome.

This is an interactive design prototype with sample data. It does not read or modify your sessions. Replay, summaries, and layouts here describe the proposed experience.
